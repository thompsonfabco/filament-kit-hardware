# Station webcam bill of materials

Fit one of these three cameras. "Reference build: yes" marks a listing used in the reference build; "suggested" marks
a listing chosen to match the part. Whichever is fitted, the bench checklist in the [guide](guide.md) checks that it
gives 1920x1080 in MJPEG.

| Ref | Part | Qty | Brand / listing | Link | Reference build | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CAM1, option 1 | USB camera module, 4MP, UVC | 1 | innomaker 4MP USB HDR camera | [B0H36XDQY8](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H36XDQY8) | yes | The listing gives MJPEG and 1080p at 60 fps. A bare 32 x 32 mm board with four M2 holes, so it needs a mount |
| CAM1, option 2 | Logitech USB webcam, 1080p or better | 1 | Logitech C920x HD Pro, 1080p/30 fps | [B085TFF7M1](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B085TFF7M1) | suggested | Any current Logitech USB webcam at 1080p or better will do; this is one listing. The listing doesn't name MJPEG: check it on the bench |
| CAM1, option 3 | USB webcam, 4K, with phase-detect autofocus | 1 | AOC 4K webcam with PDAF | [B0H6P2DWXD](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H6P2DWXD) | yes | The listing gives 1080p at 60 fps but doesn't name MJPEG: check it on the bench |
